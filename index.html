To find the address, open Arduino IDE Serial Monitor at 115200 baud and reset the ESP32-CAM. Look for the IP address printed after Wi-Fi connects. The farmer's phone or computer must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the camera. The saved address is stored in this browser on this device.
